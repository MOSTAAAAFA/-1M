Minimum Size

Minimum reproduction width: 30mm for horizontal lockup, 15mm for monogram. Never render below these dimensions.

Clear Space

Maintain a clear zone equal to the height of the "M" character on all sides. No text, imagery, or graphic elements may enter this space.

Placement

Always align to grid. Prefer top-left for documents, center for hero applications. Never place on busy backgrounds without sufficient contrast.
